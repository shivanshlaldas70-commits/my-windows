Learn before you act

Windows recovery guide

A general educational overview — exact steps depend on your device, Windows version, and situation.

⚠ Back up files before recoverySome reset, reinstall, partition, or formatting choices can permanently erase data. Back up important files, verify the official instructions for your exact situation, and do not continue if you are unsure.
1

Pause and identify the problem

Write down the error message and what changed. If Windows still starts, avoid drastic steps until you understand the issue. Do not assume a reinstall is necessary.

2

Protect important files

Back up documents, photos, projects, browser/export data, and other essentials to a trusted location. Confirm the backup can be opened from another device if possible.

3

Record your device details

Note the manufacturer, exact model, Windows edition/version, and 32-bit/64-bit/ARM system type. Keep any recovery key information in a secure place.

4

Use trusted recovery information

Start with Microsoft Support or your laptop manufacturer's official support page. Match every instruction to your device and Windows version; avoid unofficial driver bundles.

5

Understand the recovery option

Startup repair, system restore, reset, and reinstall have different effects. Read each screen carefully and verify whether personal files, apps, or partitions may be affected before confirming.

6

After Windows starts

Check Windows Update, network access, Device Manager, sound, display, and other essentials. Install model-specific drivers only from trusted official sources, then verify your backup is intact.

The startup idea

Recovery should feel less overwhelming.

RebootReady is a proposed Windows recovery companion designed to help everyday users prepare before a laptop problem becomes an emergency.

⚡

The problem

When Windows becomes corrupted or drivers stop working, users may not know their exact laptop model, where to find safe drivers, how to create recovery media, or how to protect their files.

✦

The proposed solution

One friendly dashboard for device details, a user-controlled readiness checklist, official support links, and clear recovery education — with safety warnings where they matter.

◎

Intended users

Students, first-time PC owners, families, and small teams who want to prepare for Windows problems without needing advanced technical knowledge.

↗

Prototype boundary

This demo does not detect hardware, verify backups, scan drivers, repair Windows, or install software. Real system integrations would require careful engineering, testing, permissions, and security review.
